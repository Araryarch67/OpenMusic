hrir_kemar_48k.wav (Virtual speakers) is made by tool/make_hrir.sh from the
diffuse-field equalized HRTF measurements of a KEMAR dummy-head microphone:

  Bill Gardner and Keith Martin, "HRTF Measurements of a KEMAR Dummy-Head
  Microphone", MIT Media Lab Perceptual Computing Technical Report #280, 1994.
  https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR.html
